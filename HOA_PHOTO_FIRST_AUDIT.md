# HOA Maintenance Pro Photo-First Audit

## Decision

HOA Maintenance Pro remains in scope when every record is created by, requests,
organizes, compares, verifies, or reports photographic evidence. It must not
become a general HOA accounting, resident, directory, voting, or property
management system.

## Keep and emphasize

- Asset identity and condition photo histories
- Guided inspection routes with required photographic views
- Maintenance records beginning with an issue or inspection photo
- Initial, inspection, estimate, work-in-progress, completion, final
  verification, and follow-up photo stages
- Contractor completion-photo request links
- Before/after comparison, evidence history, markup, location, and timestamps
- Board-ready reports whose findings link back to supporting photographs

## Supporting context only

Community, assignee, priority, status, budget source, cost, and board-review
fields may organize and explain photo evidence. They must not expand into a
standalone directory, accounting ledger, work-order suite, or board portal.

## Exclude

- Dues, payments, violations, elections, voting, meeting management, resident
  portals, contact databases, vendor accounting, and general CRM
- Any workflow that remains equally useful after its photographs are removed

## Feature acceptance test

Every future HOA feature must answer: which property photo does this improve,
what evidence or action does it add to that photo, and does the result remain
traceable to the original photograph?
