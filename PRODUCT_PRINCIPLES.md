# Photo Notes Pro Product Principle

## The governing rule

Every Pro edition must make a business photo more useful before, during, or
after it is captured. Photo Notes is not a general-purpose industry application
with a camera added to it. It is a photo-first application that turbocharges
photos for a specific industry's work.

The photo is the evidence, the starting point, and the center of the workflow.
Industry-specific features should add structure, context, comparison,
measurement, verification, retrieval, reporting, or action to that photo.

## Feature test

Before adding a feature to Paving Pro, HOA Maintenance Pro, or Concrete Pro,
ask:

1. What business photo does this feature improve?
2. What useful context or action does it attach to that photo?
3. Would the feature still make sense if photos were removed?

If the answer to the third question is yes, it is probably general-purpose
industry software and does not belong in Photo Notes Pro unless it directly
supports a photo-centered workflow.

## Edition direction

- **Paving Pro:** Turn paving photos into measured, classified, time-stamped,
  job-linked evidence, including delivery-ticket and extra-work documentation.
- **HOA Maintenance Pro:** Turn property photos into durable maintenance,
  inspection, condition, completion, and board-ready evidence.
- **Concrete Pro:** Turn concrete photos into placement, condition, defect,
  measurement, progress, repair, and verification evidence.

Shared functions such as projects, contacts, status, exports, and reports are
supporting context. They must remain subordinate to capturing, finding,
understanding, comparing, or acting on photos.
