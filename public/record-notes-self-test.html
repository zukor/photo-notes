Record Notes Self-Test

This test runs the real Record Notes engine ten times in a row, the same way Capture does, without reloading. For each test, tap Start Recording, read the sentence out loud at a normal pace, then tap Stop. The page checks whether your words arrived and records the result. Nothing is saved as a PhotoNote.

The result of each test, without your words or audio, is sent to PhotoNotes so the team can see it. Run it once in Safari or Chrome, and once in the Home Screen app if you use one.
