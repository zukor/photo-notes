Install Photo Notes

Add a Photo Notes icon to your phone or computer. No app store account or developer membership is needed. Your subscription and authorized versions use the same Photo Notes account.

After installation

  1. Open the new icon and sign in. Allow camera, microphone and location only when you want to use them.
  2. Take a test photo and tap Save. A local save message means the capture is waiting to upload. Wait for the upload confirmation, then verify the saved result in your account’s available workflow.
  3. Close Photo Notes and reopen its icon. Check that your authorized version is correct and the saved capture is still available.

Connection and pending photos

Keep Photo Notes open and connected while uploading. In Account menu, choose Pending Photos to check captures waiting in this browser and download a backup. Switch to a capture’s original version to upload it.

Reopening while offline may require reconnecting before sign-in works. Recording speech, AI tools, reports and sharing may also need a connection. Road Issue Reporter and scanning tools require a connection; the Pending Photos queue covers the Capture page Save workflow. Installing an icon does not make every feature available offline.

Use a normal browser window. Private browsing, clearing site data, removing the web app, device loss or storage cleanup can remove local pending files. Different browsers and installed web apps can have separate local storage. Finish pending uploads before changing browsers.
