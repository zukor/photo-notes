PhotoNotes Measuring Card

Lay this card flat on the pavement beside a pothole before using Precision Measure. PhotoNotes finds the card in the camera view, coaches you to the right angle and distance, and measures from it.

  1. Print at 100% / Actual Size. Do not choose "Fit to page" or "Shrink to fit".
  2. Measure the black square with a ruler. Each side should be exactly . If it is not, enter the size you measured in PhotoNotes under Card Settings.
  3. Glue it flat onto stiff cardboard or foam board. A curled or bent card lowers accuracy.
  4. Keep it clean and dry. Do not cover the white border around the black square.
