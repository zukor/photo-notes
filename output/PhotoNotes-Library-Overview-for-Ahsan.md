# PhotoNotes Library Workflow Overview for Ahsan

Ahsan,

Thank you for taking the time to prepare the five interactive workflow recommendations. They were very helpful, especially the recommendation to make saved photos easier to find and their actions easier to access. We used your work as additional input while completing the broader PhotoNotes workflow review.

The former **Organize** module is now **Library**. Editing is also inside Library, so users no longer have to decide whether to start in Organize or Edit.

The Library workflow now works this way:

1. Users browse a compact grid of saved PhotoNotes.
2. Search, filters, saved searches, projects, and multi-photo actions are available without dominating the photo list.
3. Each photo preview has one clear **Open** action.
4. Opening a PhotoNote reveals its editing and record actions, including photo editing, note editing, comments, related photos, favorites, flags, details and history, specialty tools where applicable, and deletion.
5. Photo selection is reserved for actions that make sense across several photos, such as project assignment, topics, comparison, documents, and supported batch changes.
6. Scheduled photography is now called **Scheduled Photos** and is available directly from Library. A schedule requires a reason, supports optional instructions, recurring dates, in-app and optional push reminders, and a custom advance-reminder period.

Your other recommendations will be handled as follows:

- **Capture:** The current approved Capture workflow remains the reference. We will not replace it with an older concept, but your Photo Reason focus will be retained where it fits the approved screen.
- **Organize:** Incorporated into the new Library workflow described above.
- **Edit:** Incorporated inside Library. The focused photo editor and its save and undo behavior remain part of the detailed PhotoNote workflow.
- **Create:** Your recommendations will be considered in the next workflow review, especially clearer document-building and review steps.
- **Send:** Send remains a separate module. Your simpler export and sharing sequence will be considered when that module is reviewed.

Thank you again. The recommendations were specific, thoughtful, and useful, and each one now has a defined place in the redesign process.
